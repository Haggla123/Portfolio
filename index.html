ABOUT ME

From building systems to understanding how to secure them.

I am a final-year Information Technology student at the University of Energy and Natural Resources (UENR) in Sunyani, Ghana, graduating in 2026.

My interests sit at the intersection of software development and cybersecurity. I enjoy building real-world systems and examining the security and privacy challenges that arise from authentication, data handling, system architecture, and digital transactions.

My projects have given me practical experience with authentication, role-based access control, hashing, HMAC-based verification, databases, APIs, real-time systems, and full-stack application development.

I am particularly interested in pursuing graduate research in cybersecurity and secure systems, while remaining open to related areas of computer science and software engineering.
